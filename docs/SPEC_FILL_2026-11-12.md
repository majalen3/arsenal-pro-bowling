# Spec fill research and the Add a ball source chain (2026-11-12)

Status: **research branch `spec-fill`, not merged, nothing in `maker_rows.json` changed.** Owner asks: pt138u (find the missing specs legally) and t139u (the same chain must run inside Add a ball once a name is picked).

## Legal source order (both the catalogue research and the in-app lookup)
1. Maker's current page or spec sheet. 2. Archived maker page (Wayback availability API, then the `…id_/` raw capture). 3. USBC Approved Ball List (bowl.com): approval date and maker only. 4. Allowlisted public secondary pages that republish the maker table, labelled secondary. 5. Forums: never fetched by code; the app offers "type the specs from the box".
Never used, in research or code: bowlingballvideos, bowwwl, bowlingball.com, BowlingThisMonth (hard-coded `BANNED` in the API; the allowlist check rejects them even inside an archive URL).

## What stays missing, and why
- Polyester spare balls (Brunswick Viz-a-Ball, TZone etc., Hammer Axe): the maker info sheets and ball talkers print no RG, differential or core numbers.
- KR Strikeforce Rise x5: KR prints no RG, diff or symmetry. Only third-party numbers exist; CarryDown's equal the banned bowwwl's, so they are listed as unverified in `spec_fill.json` and not applied.
- Honey Badger: the tech sheet is not in the Wayback Machine.
- Symmetry on several Brunswick, Ebonite, DV8 and Hammer rows: the core name is printed but not symmetric/asymmetric. Not guessed. (Sibling-core candidates, e.g. Hustle Ink, are listed, not applied.)
- Epic, SWAG: pages print no RG/diff/release; their terms bar automated access.

## Terms findings
- BowlersMart, Epic Bowling and SWAG terms bar bots/scrapers without permission. Two BowlersMart pages were fetched before the terms were read; deleted, nothing used. Epic's 23 pages had been fetched before the terms were read; nothing new was extracted, no further requests. No source with these terms is on the in-app allowlist.
- Hammer and KR (Shopify) publish an agents.md asking agent traffic to identify with Web Bot Auth; our reads used an honest user-agent only. Low volume, but a decision for the owner before any bulk use.
- bowl.com robots allows the Approved Ball List; the USBC file is read once and shipped as data, not fetched per user.

## In-app lookup (Add a ball) — design
`/api/spec-lookup` (arsenal-pro-api, branch `spec-chain`) runs maker → archived maker → USBC → allowlisted secondary. Later steps only fill what is still missing; disagreements come back as `conflicts`. Each field carries `src.type` (maker | archived-maker | usbc | secondary); the app shows a chip per field, a "Checked: …" line, and a "check against the box" note for anything not from the maker's current page. Nothing is saved until the user taps Add. Rate limits: per-host minimum gaps (Wayback 2.5 s, Wikipedia 1.5 s, USBC/others 4 s), robots.txt with Crawl-delay, 18 s chain budget, cache key `v3:`.
Code: `js/cc/addball.js` (`fromLookup`), `js/screens/addball.js`, `css/app.css`; tests `tests/data/addball.test.mjs` (12), `tests/addball_chain_smoke.py` (Playwright, mocked API).
